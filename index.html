From the field to the data field.

I was always the kid on the field. Sports before studies, every single time. A career in data? Never even crossed my mind.

Plot twist: here we are.

At 23, after completing my BBA, I started my corporate journey as a Project Coordinator, working with clients, internal teams, and a famously grumpy manager who also happened to be the CEO. Fun times. Somewhere between tracking certification timelines, compliance, and risks across cybersecurity projects, I learned how numbers keep a business honest.

Next came my role as Pre-Sales Analyst, where I analyzed healthcare market datasets, conducted secondary research, built trackers and performance dashboards, and drafted proposals and SOWs. I got a closer look at how businesses operate together and what stakeholders actually need from their data.

But across both roles, I kept gravitating towards the same things: reports, dashboards, and figuring out the why behind the numbers. I just didn't know it was an actual career until I discovered Data and Business Analytics.

So, I left my job and started learning the technical side. I'd already acquired the business sense; I just needed to learn how to put it into practice.

Today, I'm building my skills in data analytics, turning messy datasets into meaningful insights, and figuring out what the numbers are really trying to say. Turns out, reading a game and reading data aren't all that different. You spot patterns, anticipate the next move, and figure out what actually changes the outcome.

The playing field changed. The mindset didn't. Just like every great player studies the game, I study the data behind everything.
